# 📊 SuperStore US 2015 – Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📁 Project Contents

## 📌 Objective

To analyze SuperStore’s sales performance across various dimensions such as:

- Regions
- Product categories
- Managers
- Shipping modes
- Discounts
- Cities and states
- Monthly trends

And to **convert raw data into meaningful insights** that can help drive business decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – For data modeling and dashboard creation
- **Microsoft Excel** – Initial data source (cleaned and loaded in Power BI)
- **DAX** (Data Analysis Expressions) – For creating calculated fields and measures

## 🔍 Step-by-Step Process

### 1. 🧼 Data Preparation

- Used the dataset: `P1-SuperStoreUS-2015.xlsx`
- Loaded 3 sheets: `Orders`, `Returns`, and `Users`
- Cleaned the data: removed null columns like `Unnamed: 26`
- Converted dates into readable format (`Order Date`, `Ship Date`)
- Created calculated column for:
  - Month Name

---

### 2. 📊 Dashboard Design

Created an **interactive Power BI dashboard** containing:

#### 👉 KPIs (Cards)
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Ordered

#### 👉 Visuals
- Bar Chart: Sales by State, Manager, Category
- Column Chart: Discounts by Product Category
- Map: Regional Sales
- Line Chart: Monthly Profit Trends
- Donut Chart: Shipping Cost by Ship Mode
- Average Unit Price by Product Category
- Slicers for:
  - Product Category
  - Month
  - Region
  - Ship Mode

#### 🎯 Summary Page
- A final page combining insights and recommended actions.

---

## 📈 Business Insights

1. **California and New York** generate the highest sales.
2. **East Region** is the most profitable.
3. **Erin** is the top-performing manager.
4. **Office Supplies** have the highest discounts but lowest profits.
5. **South Region** is underperforming—needs attention.

---

## ✅ Recommended Actions

- Reduce unnecessary discounts on Office Supplies.
- Use Erin’s sales approach as a model across teams.
- Focus marketing and logistics improvement in the South Region.
- Target high-profit cities like Los Angeles and New York for new campaigns.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- Hands-on experience in dashboard storytelling using Power BI.
- Improved data visualization and filtering techniques.
- Learned how to convert raw sales data into actionable insights.
- Understood how to communicate findings through summary pages.
